## 💼 What I Build

### 🏥 Hospital Management Systems
Patient records, appointments, billing, and staff scheduling — built for real hospitals with role-based access for doctors, nurses, admins, and reception.

### ⚖️ Court Management Systems
Case tracking, hearing schedules, judge and lawyer workflows, document management, and report generation for judicial institutions.

### 🎓 Research Management Systems
Proposal submission, ethical review workflows, grant project tracking, publication management, and analytics dashboards for research offices.

### 🚌 Bus Ticketing Systems
Route management, seat booking, ticket issuance, agent tracking, and daily sales reports — with offline-capable interfaces for ticket counters.

### 🌐 WordPress Websites
Custom themes and plugins, business sites, and content-driven platforms for organizations that need a fast, manageable web presence.
